Two questions for the same board

Stockfish searches variations to evaluate move quality. Maia predicts a distribution of human moves at a selected rating. Their disagreement can reveal tempting alternatives worth studying.

Read the numbers correctly

A Maia candidate marked 30% means predicted move choice, not a 30% chance to win. Stockfish scores in the studio use White’s perspective: positive favours White, negative favours Black.

Test your own idea

Import a study FEN or a completed game, choose an intuition rating, and compare the candidates. Play your own variation on the board, then return to the original game.

Understand the limits

Maia is a population-level prediction model, not a personalized model of your playing style. Brilliant and intuition-trap labels are Diverge heuristics, dependent on engine depth, legal alternatives and Maia predictions; they are not official platform ratings.

Turn a diagram into a position

Find a static chess image on a webpage, scan it, check the pieces and side to move, then explore.

Compare two ways of choosing

Stockfish evaluates chess strength. Maia predicts moves people at a selected rating are likely to play.

Review a finished game

Import PGN or a supported public completed-game link. Follow the evaluation chart and explore your own variations.

Built for study and post-game review

Active games lock analysis, candidate moves, arrows and heatmaps. Use a supported analysis board, a static study diagram or a completed game. Unknown interactive game sources stay locked.
