Start small. Explore at your pace.

The extension includes Stockfish 19 Lite and a small local diagram recognition model. Maia 5M downloads about 28 MiB on first use and is cached in your browser; 23M and 79M are optional. No account is required to use the studio. Screenshots are processed locally. Public game imports contact the relevant chess platform.

Built for study and post-game review

Active games lock analysis, candidate moves, arrows and heatmaps. Use a supported analysis board, a static study diagram or a completed game. Unknown interactive game sources stay locked.
