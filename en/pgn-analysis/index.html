Choose an input

Open the studio and import a PGN file, paste a complete PGN, or enter a supported public Chess.com or Lichess completed-game link. A clear final result is required for game review.

Follow the game

Review engine accuracy estimates, the evaluation chart, notation and key moves. Analysis can take time; a partial or failed evaluation is not a complete assessment.

Explore variations

Navigate with the arrow keys or move pieces on the board. Right-drag draws thought arrows. Your study variation stays separate from the played game.

For a single position

Paste FEN to study a position. A FEN contains no full-game history, so game accuracy and the full-game chart are not shown. Private/live game links cannot be used for assistance.

Turn a diagram into a position

Find a static chess image on a webpage, scan it, check the pieces and side to move, then explore.

Compare two ways of choosing

Stockfish evaluates chess strength. Maia predicts moves people at a selected rating are likely to play.

Review a finished game

Import PGN or a supported public completed-game link. Follow the evaluation chart and explore your own variations.

Built for study and post-game review

Active games lock analysis, candidate moves, arrows and heatmaps. Use a supported analysis board, a static study diagram or a completed game. Unknown interactive game sources stay locked.
