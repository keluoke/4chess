Scan a visible diagram

Install the extension, open a webpage with a complete static chess diagram, and choose Scan page from the toolbar popup. If automatic detection fails, select a crop and try again.

Check before you calculate

Recognition can make mistakes. Check all pieces, viewing orientation and side to move. Use Edit position or paste a corrected FEN, then confirm before analysis starts.

Explore or copy FEN

Click or drag pieces to explore legal moves, see up to three Stockfish Lite lines, or copy the FEN. Open the studio to compare the current position with Maia human move predictions.

What an image cannot tell you

A single diagram does not recover game history, castling rights or en passant rights. Image positions default to no castling or en passant; side to move may need correction. Live games and unknown interactive sources are excluded.

Turn a diagram into a position

Find a static chess image on a webpage, scan it, check the pieces and side to move, then explore.

Compare two ways of choosing

Stockfish evaluates chess strength. Maia predicts moves people at a selected rating are likely to play.

Review a finished game

Import PGN or a supported public completed-game link. Follow the evaluation chart and explore your own variations.

Built for study and post-game review

Active games lock analysis, candidate moves, arrows and heatmaps. Use a supported analysis board, a static study diagram or a completed game. Unknown interactive game sources stay locked.
